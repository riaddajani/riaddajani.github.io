Mission

My work centers on enhancing cognitive bandwidth—building models and systems that help propel understanding, empathy, and consciousness. I care about useful intelligence: research that translates into tools, and tools that continuously learn from how people actually use them. That mission links everything I build: production ML for human–computer interaction at Superpower Social, and research on brain/behavior–aligned models at Isik Lab.

Featured Projects

BrainToken in progress

Neural-to-text decoding via JEPA and perplexity-guided learning. Translates ECoG brain signals into natural language using soft prompts that condition a frozen LLM.

Superpower Social

AI moderation and auto-engagement platform serving 300+ brands and agencies. Blocked millions of harmful comments across Instagram, Facebook, and Shopify with 24/7 automated moderation, ~83% reduction in LLM inference costs, and 10x faster response generation vs. manual workflows.

BrainLang

Decoding heard language from fMRI into text space. Early versions explore BiLSTM + attention with dimensionality-reduced inputs and continuous decoding; current work expands training stability and evaluation.

Pet Projects

Foundational cognitive science models rebuilt from scratch: Hodgkin-Huxley dynamics, Hopfield networks, perceptron learning, delta rule, and damped oscillators. Understanding the primitives of signal, memory, and stability.

Extreme Theory: Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks unpublished

Rigorous theoretical framework applying extreme theory to KANs. Derives laws and predictions about interpretability, efficiency, and robustness from the axiomatic foundations of the Kolmogorov-Arnold representation theorem.

VoxDigit

Real-time spoken digit recognition with early-exit, few-shot enrollment, and interpretability. 99.7% accuracy on CPU with millisecond latency—built for practical, low-resource deployment.

Community Notes + SIGReg in progress

Isotropic Gaussian regularization for Community Notes matrix factorization. Adding Epps-Pulley test (O(n), bounded gradients) and random projection sketching for scalable, robust note scoring.
